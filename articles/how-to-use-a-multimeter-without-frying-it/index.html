How to Use a Multimeter (Without Frying It)

Mar 30, 2026 · 5 min read · By ElectricTechs Team
A multimeter is a flashlight for electricity. Learn five readings and you'll diagnose 90% of problems in your house.

Voltage AC and DC

Set the dial, probe across the load (parallel). Red to live/positive, black to neutral/ground. Never in series — that's how you blow the fuse.

Continuity

The beep test. Ideal for tracing wires, checking switches and finding broken connections. Always with the circuit de-energized.

Resistance and Current

Resistance for component testing. Current measurements go in series and require moving the red probe to the dedicated A jack — a common, costly mistake.

⚠️ Safety first: Electrical work can be dangerous. Always turn off power at the breaker, verify with a tester, and follow local codes. When in doubt, call a licensed electrician.
