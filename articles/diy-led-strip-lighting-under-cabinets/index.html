DIY LED Strip Lighting Under Cabinets

Apr 08, 2026 · 6 min read · By ElectricTechs Team
Stick-on LED strips look great for a week, then sag. Here's how to do it properly with an aluminum channel and a hidden driver.

Pick the Right Strip

24V strips have less voltage drop over long runs. CRI 90+ for kitchens, 2700K for warm or 4000K for task lighting.

Hide the Driver

Mount the LED driver inside a nearby cabinet with ventilation. Run low-voltage DC through a small hole to the channel below.

Wire the Switch

Use a wall-mounted dimmer with PWM output rated for your driver. Avoid leading-edge dimmers — they'll flicker.

⚠️ Safety first: Electrical work can be dangerous. Always turn off power at the breaker, verify with a tester, and follow local codes. When in doubt, call a licensed electrician.
