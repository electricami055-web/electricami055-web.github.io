Wiring Basics

House Wiring 101: A Complete Beginner's Guide

May 02, 2026 · 8 min read · By ElectricTechs Team
Residential wiring looks intimidating, but every system follows the same simple logic: a source, a path, a load, and protection. Master those four ideas and the rest is just detail.

Live, Neutral and Earth Explained

Live carries current to the load, neutral returns it to the panel, and earth is the safety path that trips the breaker if something goes wrong. Color codes vary by region — always verify locally before working.

Choosing the Right Wire Gauge

Use 14 AWG for 15 A lighting circuits, 12 AWG for 20 A outlet circuits, and 10 AWG for 30 A appliances. Undersized wire is the #1 cause of overheating in DIY work.

Reading a Simple Circuit

A circuit diagram is just a map of how electrons travel. Start at the breaker, follow the live wire to the switch, then to the load, and back via neutral. If you can trace it, you can troubleshoot it.

Tools You Actually Need

A non-contact voltage tester, a quality multimeter, wire strippers, lineman's pliers and a torque screwdriver will handle 95% of home jobs safely.

⚠️ Safety first: Electrical work can be dangerous. Always turn off power at the breaker, verify with a tester, and follow local codes. When in doubt, call a licensed electrician.
